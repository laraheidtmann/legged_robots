What is the meaning of the variables h?

-> the timestep size between two steps, but its a decision variable. The solver is free to choose the timestep between an upper and a lower limit, 500Hz and 100Hz. 

What is the meaning of the term S*sum(h) ?

It penalizes high times to reach the goal. The solver is urged ti reach the goal as fast as possible.

What happens if we add constraints on the magnitude of u(t)?

--> if the constraint doesnt allow high engough torques, the stick doesnt reach the top. If it doesnt allow low enogh torques (e.g. >4), it overshoots and cant find the stability.

What is the difference of this pendulum problem compared to the CoM trajectory optimization
(hint look at their dynamics equations)?

-->The key difference is in the dynamics equation.
In the LIPM the CoM acceleration depends on the position of the CoM itself, making it an unstable system that wants to fall over. 
You control it indirectly through the ZMP p.
In a simple CoM trajectory optimization it's just a double integrator where you directly command acceleration.
So the LIPM is fundamentally harder, since the system is unstable and you can only influence the CoM indirectly through where you place your feet.
