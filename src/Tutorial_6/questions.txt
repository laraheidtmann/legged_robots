What is the meaning of the variables h?
-> the timestep size between two steps, but its a decision variable. The solver is free to choose the timestep between an upper and a lower limit, 500Hz and 100Hz. 
What is the meaning of the term S*sum(h) ?
It penalizes high times to reach the goal. The solver is urged ti reach the goal as fast as possible.

What happens if we add constraints on the magnitude of u(t)?
--> if the constraint doesnt allow high engough torques, the stick doesnt reach the top. If it doesnt allow low enogh torques (e.g. >4), it overshoots and cant find the stability.
